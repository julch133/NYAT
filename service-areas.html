Service-area questions

Not sure whether your location is covered?

Do you only work with businesses in Savannah?

No. Not Your Average Tech is based in Savannah and serves nearby communities, regional markets within roughly 200 miles, and remote clients across the United States.

Do you travel to every location listed?

Some nearby projects can include local meetings. Markets farther from Savannah are generally served remotely unless an in-person need is discussed and approved before the project begins.

What work can be completed remotely?

Website design, content updates, spreadsheet cleanup, custom tracking tools, data organization, workflow reviews, SOPs, forms, and many technology-integration projects can be completed online.

What if my town is not listed?

Contact Not Your Average Tech with your location and the type of help you need. Nearby towns may still be covered, and most digital projects are not limited by distance.
