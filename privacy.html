Information collected

When you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, message, and—if you choose—your phone number. The form also sends the page address and your browser's user-agent information to help identify the source and technical context of the request.

The website may also generate standard technical records through hosting or third-party infrastructure, such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, and date and time of access.

This website does not intentionally collect payment-card information or sensitive personal information through its contact form.

How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Respond to questions and service inquiries.
  • Provide requested services and communicate about potential or active projects.
  • Maintain website security, troubleshoot problems, and understand how the website is reached and used.
  • Meet legal, accounting, or recordkeeping obligations.

Sharing and service providers

Personal information is not sold or rented. It may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, email, and business operations.

The contact form is submitted through Google Apps Script, so Google may process the form data on behalf of Not Your Average Tech under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including follow-up, business records, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact Not Your Average Tech to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted.

Some information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate business records. You may also choose not to use the contact form and instead call or email directly.

Children's privacy

This business website is not directed to children under 13, and personal information from children under 13 is not knowingly collected.

If you believe a child has submitted information, please get in touch so it can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or business practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will show when revisions were made.
