Frequently asked questions

Remote project questions

What can be completed without an in-person meeting?

Most website, spreadsheet, tracking, documentation, data-cleanup, and workflow projects can be completed remotely. Hardware and hands-on local troubleshooting may require a nearby provider.

How do remote projects stay organized?

The project is divided into clear stages for discovery, content or data collection, review, revisions, approval, and handoff. The exact communication plan is included in the scope.

Do you serve every industry?

Many services apply across industries, but fit is evaluated project by project. Regulated, highly specialized, or enterprise work may require additional experts or controls.
